Hough Green Park Bowls Club

About Us

Are you over 55, retired and looking for something to do? Are you are interested in playing: dominoes, darts, cards, snooker or crown green bowling, or just having a chat over a cup of tea and putting the world to right? If you are, then Hough Green Park Pensioner’s club may be just the place for you. The friendly club is located in the centre of Hough Green Park. Summer Outdoor League bowls and indoor actives all year round.
